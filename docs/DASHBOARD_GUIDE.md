# Dashboard User Guide

The platform has four role-based dashboards plus shared pages (Inbox, Notifications, Settings, Profile). Log in with a seeded account (see `INSTALLATION_GUIDE.md`) to access each.

## Admin Dashboard (`/dashboard/admin`)

- **Dashboard** — platform-wide stats (users, marketing teams, creators, connected accounts, running campaigns, scheduled/published posts), recent registrations, quick actions, platform activity
- **Users** — manage all platform users, roles and access; add users
- **Marketing Teams** — manage marketing teams and members
- **Content Creators** — manage creator accounts
- **Analytics** — KPI cards (impressions, engagement rate, link clicks, new followers), performance analytics, top performing posts; **Generate Report** opens a printable platform-wide report
- **Reports** — latest platform summary, generated reports list with download/delete
- **Notifications** — platform and account notifications
- **Settings** — account and notification preferences

## Business Dashboard (`/dashboard/business`)

- Workspace overview, client–team assignment and marketing activity
- Campaign tracking, connected accounts, scheduled/published posts
- Team requests (request/remove a marketing team), work requests with brand guidelines
- Reports and analytics for the business

## Marketing Dashboard (`/dashboard/marketing`)

- **Clients** — workspace list with per-client content and campaign management
- **Requests** — incoming work requests (approve/reject) and team connection requests
- **Content** — content management, library, review, scheduling
- **Scheduling / Calendar** — publishing calendar and content scheduling
- **Campaigns** — client campaign creation and management
- **Publishing** — queue, logs, failed posts (retry), platform history
- **Reports & Analytics** — client-level reporting and analytics
- **Notifications** — work-request and collaboration notifications

## Creator Dashboard (`/dashboard/creator`)

- **Dashboard** — recent posts, notifications, quick actions
- **My Posts** — create, edit, delete posts; drafts, scheduled, published
- **Content Scheduling** — schedule posts across connected accounts
- **Publishing Calendar** — visual schedule of posts
- **Publishing** — queue, logs, failed posts, platform history
- **Campaigns** — create campaigns, assign posts, timeline, analytics
- **Social Accounts** — connect/disconnect Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Pinterest
- **Analytics** — content, audience, campaign, platform comparison, performance trends (with date/platform/campaign/content-type filters)
- **Notifications / Profile / Settings**

## Shared Pages

- **Inbox** — unified inbox overview and messages
- **Notifications** — notification center with filters (all/unread/success/error/campaign/info), mark-read, clear-all
- **Profile** — edit personal information and avatar
- **Settings** — account, security, privacy, region and notification preferences

## Reporting

- **Generate Report** (Analytics / Reports pages) opens a printable platform-wide report with KPIs, platform breakdown, and top posts
- **Reports page** lists generated reports with download (PDF) and delete actions
